Privacy Policy

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 5 日

Effective Date: September 5, 2026

摘要（先看这里）

鸣静不联网、不收集、不上传。你的所有数据（日记、急救记录、声疗设置等）只保存在你自己的 iPhone 上。本应用没有账号系统、没有统计SDK、没有广告、没有服务器。

Summary (Read This First)

MingJing (Tinnitus Relief) does not connect to any server, collect, or upload data. All of your data (diary, spike records, sound therapy settings, etc.) stays on your own iPhone. This app has no accounts, no analytics SDKs, no ads, and no servers.

一、我们不收集任何信息

鸣静是一款纯本地工具。我们不会收集、记录、传输或分享以下任何内容：

  • 姓名、邮箱、电话等个人身份信息（应用无需注册，无账号系统）
  • 你的耳鸣日记、急救使用记录、匹配的特征频率
  • 设备标识符、IP 地址、位置信息
  • 使用行为数据、崩溃统计（未接入任何统计或崩溃上报 SDK）

应用不会请求网络权限进行数据传输；它播放声音不需要连接互联网。

1. We Collect Nothing

MingJing is a fully offline tool. We do not collect, log, transmit, or share any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers such as name, email, or phone number (the app requires no sign-up and has no account system)
  • Your tinnitus diary, spike records, or matched characteristic frequency
  • Device identifiers, IP address, or location
  • Usage analytics or crash statistics (no analytics or crash-reporting SDK is integrated)

The app never transmits data over the network; playing sounds does not require an internet connection.

二、数据只存在你的设备上

你在应用内创建的所有内容（日记、记录、偏好设置、保存的声景配方）均使用 iOS 系统提供的本地存储保存在你的设备中，开发者无法读取。

  • 删除即销毁：卸载应用会永久删除以上全部本地数据。
  • 备份完全由你掌控：应用提供的"导出备份"功能会把数据生成一个 JSON 文件保存到你选择的位置（如"文件"App），整个过程不经过任何服务器；"导入恢复"同样只读取你亲自选择的本地文件。

2. Data Lives Only on Your Device

Everything you create in the app (diary entries, records, preferences, saved soundscape presets) is stored locally on your device using iOS system storage. The developer has no way to read it.

  • Delete means destroy: Uninstalling the app permanently removes all local data listed above.
  • Backups are fully under your control: The "Export Backup" feature generates a JSON file saved to a location you choose (such as the Files app). Nothing passes through any server. "Import Restore" likewise only reads local files you personally select.

三、购买与付款

应用内购买（会员订阅/买断）完全通过 Apple 的 App Store 与 StoreKit 处理。我们无法接触你的支付账号、银行卡或 Apple ID 信息；退款事宜请直接联系 Apple 支持。

3. Purchases & Payments

In-app purchases (membership subscription / lifetime buyout) are processed entirely through Apple's App Store and StoreKit. We never touch your payment account, bank card, or Apple ID details. For refunds, please contact Apple Support directly.

四、系统权限的使用

  • 音频播放：用于播放声疗与环境声，属应用核心功能。
  • 文件选择（仅当你使用导入备份时）：仅读取你亲自挑选的那一个备份文件，不做任何其他访问。

鸣静不请求相机、麦克风、定位、通讯录、健康数据等任何其他权限。

4. System Permissions

  • Audio playback: Used for sound therapy and ambient sounds — the core function of the app.
  • File picker (only when you use Import Backup): Reads only the single backup file you select; no other access.

MingJing does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, health data, or any other permissions.

五、儿童隐私

本应用不针对 13 岁以下儿童营销，也不收集任何用户的数据——因此同样不收集儿童数据。家长无需担心儿童使用本应用产生的任何信息泄露问题。

5. Children's Privacy

This app is not marketed to children under 13 and collects no data from any users — including children. Parents need not worry about any information leakage from a child's use of this app.

六、无第三方 SDK

鸣静未集成任何广告、统计、追踪或社交类第三方 SDK，不存在向第三方共享数据的行为。

6. No Third-Party SDKs

MingJing integrates no third-party SDK for advertising, analytics, tracking, or social features, and shares no data with any third party.

七、你的权利

由于所有数据都在你的设备上，你天然拥有完整控制权：

  • 查看与修改：随时在应用内查看、编辑或删除任何记录。
  • 可携带：通过"导出备份"随时带走全部数据。
  • 彻底删除：卸载应用即永久删除一切数据，无需向我们申请。

7. Your Rights

Because all data lives on your device, you inherently have full control:

  • Access & correction: View, edit, or delete any record at any time within the app.
  • Portability: Take all your data with you anytime via "Export Backup".
  • Complete erasure: Uninstalling the app permanently erases everything — no request to us needed.

若未来应用功能发生重大变化（例如引入联网服务），我们将在本页面更新政策并修改生效日期，且届时会重新征求你的同意。建议你定期查阅本页面。

8. Changes to This Policy

If the app's functionality changes significantly in the future (for example, introducing online services), we will update this page, revise the effective date, and seek your consent anew at that time. Please check this page periodically.
